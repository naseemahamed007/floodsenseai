Disaster guidance

Find preparedness information and official disaster-management guidance.

National Disaster Management Authority ↗

Before heavy rain

  • Monitor official weather alerts and local authority announcements.
  • Charge your phone and keep essential medicines and documents accessible.
  • Know safe routes and nearby designated shelters.
  • Avoid unnecessary travel through waterlogged areas.

During flooding

  • Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater.
  • Keep away from exposed electrical equipment and fallen power lines.
  • Follow evacuation instructions from local authorities.
  • Call emergency services if anyone is in immediate danger.
